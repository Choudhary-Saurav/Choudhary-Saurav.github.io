Saurav Choudhary

University of Georgia · Ye Lab, Athens, GA

I connect genetic variation to the cell types and regulatory programs that shape complex traits, combining GWAS with single-cell multi-omics and deep learning, and I build chromosome-scale genome resources for non-model organisms.

Research interests

From raw sequencing reads to interpretable biology.

All projects →

GWAS × Single-cell Integration

Linking GWAS variants to trait-relevant cell types using single-cell multi-omics, with a focus on PUFA metabolism, and inferring gene regulatory networks with deep learning.

GWAS scRNA-seq scATAC-seq GRNs

Single-cell Genomics

Atlas-building with large single-cell datasets for cell-type discovery, stem cell heterogeneity, and spatial niches.

Cell atlases snRNA-seq Spatial

Genome Assembly

Chromosome-scale genome assembly and annotation of non-model species using long-read sequencing, including a new reference genome for the rat tapeworm.
